## About Section

I build systems that turn messy operational data into decisions someone can actually act on.

My background started in procurement and supply chain, which means I think about data problems the way an operations person does first, and an engineer second — the question is never "can I build a model," it's "will a facility manager or a CFO trust this enough to act on it."

Recent work spans:

- A supply chain intelligence platform for medical commodity distribution across Kenya's 47 counties — a financial risk scorecard, stockout forecasting, and an automated redistribution engine that matches surplus to shortage using real distance data.
- A depot turnaround system for fuel logistics that predicts clearance delays before they trigger demurrage penalties.
- Backend microservices work on live agency banking transaction systems (Java/Spring Boot).

Stack I work in day to day: Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn, FastAPI), Java/Spring Boot, React/Next.js, SQL, and the statistical tooling (hypothesis testing, SHAP, forecasting) that makes a model's output defensible in front of a non-technical audience.

Open to roles where the job is making operations measurably better — not just building a dashboard, but changing what a team does next.
